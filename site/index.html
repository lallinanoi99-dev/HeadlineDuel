FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. There are no fees or tokens. Starting a duel and voting are BOT Chain transactions, so you pay a small network gas fee.

Can people vote twice?

No. The contract stores one vote per wallet per duel, and the creator can't vote on their own duel.

Why lock the AI pick?

So the prediction can't be changed after votes come in. The pick, confidence and keccak256 hash of the reason are stored at creation; the reason text is in the creation event, and anyone can check it matches the hash.

Who can see results?

While a duel is live, results unlock after you vote (and always for the creator). Once it ends, everyone can see them. All votes are public on-chain.

Do I need a wallet to vote?

Yes — to keep it fair. One wallet, one vote. You can browse the wall and finished results without one.
