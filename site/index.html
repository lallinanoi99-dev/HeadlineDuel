FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. There are no fees or tokens. Starting a duel and voting are BOT Chain transactions, so you pay a small network fee.

Can people vote twice?

No. The contract stores one vote per wallet per duel, and the creator can't vote on their own duel.

Why lock the AI pick?

So the prediction can't change after votes come in. The pick, the confidence and a keccak256 hash of the reason are stored when the duel starts. The reason text is in the creation event, and anyone can check it matches the hash.

Who can see results?

While a duel is live, you see the results after you vote. The creator can always see them. Once the duel ends, everyone can. All votes are public on-chain.

Do I need a wallet to vote?

Yes, to keep it fair: one wallet, one vote. You can browse the wall and finished results without one.
